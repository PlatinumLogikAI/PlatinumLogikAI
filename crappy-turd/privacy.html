Crappy Turd — Privacy Policy

Effective August 28, 2026

Short version: Crappy Turd does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no network requests of any kind. The only things saved are two small preferences on your own device, and they never leave it.

What the app stores

Crappy Turd saves exactly two values using your browser's or device's local storage — never transmitted anywhere:

  • Mute preference — whether sound is on or off
  • High score — your best result, for the game's own "Best:" display

Both are stored only on your own device (via standard localStorage). Uninstalling the app or clearing site data removes them completely.

What the app does not do

  • No account creation, login, or personal information of any kind
  • No advertising or ad SDKs
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services
  • No network requests while playing — the game runs entirely offline once loaded
  • No data is shared with any third party, because none is collected in the first place

Children's privacy

Because no data is collected from anyone, this applies equally to users of all ages, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes — for example, if a future update adds an optional online leaderboard — this page will be updated first, and the "Effective" date above will change accordingly.
